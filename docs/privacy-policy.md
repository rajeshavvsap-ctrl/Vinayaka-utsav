# Privacy Policy – Vinayaka Utsav

_Last updated: 3 October 2026_

Vinayaka Utsav is a private app used by our Vinayaka Chaturthi committee to organise the festival. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

## What we collect

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Name, mobile number, email address | To create your account and let the committee admin approve you |
| Password | Used only by Firebase Authentication to sign you in; the committee cannot see it |
| Pooja seva sign-ups, activities | To plan the festival schedule |
| Expenses and contributions you enter, with bill or payment screenshots | To keep transparent committee accounts and prepare reports |

We do not collect your location, contacts or any other phone data, and we do not show ads.

## Where it is stored

All data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Only committee members approved by an admin can read the data. Admins can approve members, verify payments and download reports.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with anyone outside the committee. Admins may share the festival accounts report (PDF) with committee members.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from the app: **menu (⋮) → Delete my account** (also available on the "Waiting for approval" screen). This permanently deletes your login and profile. Expenses and contributions you entered remain part of the committee's accounts.

If you cannot open the app, email **rajeshavv.sap@gmail.com** with the subject "Delete my account" from the email address you registered with, and we will delete it within 7 days.
